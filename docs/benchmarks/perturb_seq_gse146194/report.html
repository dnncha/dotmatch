Question and method

On an accessioned multi-guide direct-capture Perturb-seq guide-enrichment run, can DotMatch reproduce independent fixed-window per-read guide-barcode assignments while keeping ambiguous and unmatched reads explicit?

The published 32-guide GBC table is extracted from Supplementary Table 2. DotMatch Hamming-radius calls at k=0 and k=1 are checked record-for-record against independent exact-slice and exhaustive Hamming oracles.

Evidence boundary

Proves: matched-rule per-read assignment and explicit QC on the checked held-out prefix. Does not prove: cell/UMI calls, expression quantification, Cell Ranger parity, perturbation effects, biological validity, full-run prevalence, or speed superiority.

Next step
