Important qualifications

  • FINITE_HYPOTHESIS_SPACE — Absence of a counterexample among declared hypotheses is not evidence of biological completeness.
  • NO_EXPERIMENTAL_VALIDATION — This alpha has software tests, not measured assay sensitivity or empirical validation.
  • EXACT_MATCH_MODEL — Both inward heteroprimer orientations are rematched on edited local sequences. Exact matching is not PCR prediction; mismatches, same-primer products and sites outside this window are not modeled.
  • SYNTHETIC_REFERENCE — This reference is synthetic demonstration data.

The model behind this report

  1. Only the explicitly declared local alleles and diploid clonal hypotheses are compared. No exhaustive biological-outcome coverage is claimed.
  2. Exact primer matches are searched on each reconstructed local allele, in both inward-facing heteroprimer orientations. Every in-bounds product contributes a sequence signal. Mismatches, same-primer amplification and nonlocal binding sites are not modeled.
  3. Every eligible product is detected without error. Real sampling, PCR bias, assay failure and limits of detection are not modeled.
  4. An assay observes the SET of full-insert sequences or ordered post-primer-trim read pairs. Allele multiplicity, read counts and genomic copy number are deliberately not observed.
  5. Configured product-size bounds are hard user-declared inclusion bounds, not a model of PCR efficiency.
  6. Paired-end readout does not observe the unsequenced gap or insert length. Full-insert mode requires genuinely observing the entire primer-trimmed insert, not merely using a long-read platform.
  7. Model counterexamples are not evidence that a defect actually occurred. No clinical or clone-release decision is authorized by this software.
